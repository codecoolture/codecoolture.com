# My library is open

After almost 15 years in this profession, I have accumulated quite a few books
that have influenced my thinking and approach to software development, as well
as how I interact with others. I believe they're worth sharing.

There is now a new page on this site showcasing all the books I consider
valuable enough to recommend (note: this is not an exhaustive list but rather a
curated selection). To make it easier to browse, I've categorized them based on
how they've shaped my thinking or how I've incorporated their ideas into my
daily work.

Without further ado, [the library](/library)!
